About Us

Our story, mission, and the team behind AADUKALAM HOLIDAYS

Our Story

AADUKALAM HOLIDAYS was founded in 2010 with a simple mission: to showcase the incredible beauty and cultural richness of Tamil Nadu to travelers from around the world.

What started as a small local tour operator has grown into one of Tamil Nadu's most trusted travel companies, known for our authentic experiences and personalized service.

Our Mission

To create unforgettable travel experiences that connect people with Tamil Nadu's heritage, nature, and communities while promoting sustainable tourism practices.
